A few answers

Good questions deserve clear answers.

How do I enroll in a course?

Create an account, choose a course, and complete our demo checkout. No real payment is taken.

Are payments real?

No. Payments are mocked for this MVP. We store the selected method and, for card demos, the last four digits only. Never enter full payment details.

How long can I access my courses?

Enrolled courses remain available in your learning library in this demo.

Will I receive a certificate?

Complete every lesson and you can view and print your certificate from your dashboard.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the forgot-password link. In development the reset URL is written to the server console.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. EduHub is designed to work on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
